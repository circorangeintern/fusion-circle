Part 2 of the ResultTrack Privacy Policy & Terms and Conditions document, covering the rules for using ResultTrack.

1. Acceptance

Using ResultTrack means accepting these terms. If you don't agree, don't use the platform, contact the School directly instead. For a minor, a parent or guardian accepts these terms on the student's behalf, the same person whose verified contact is used during registration.

2. Who Can Use ResultTrack

Access is limited to students, teachers, lecturers, and administrative staff of the School, provisioned through the roster the School itself maintains. There is no public or open registration.

3. Account Responsibilities

  • Keep your password and any activation PIN confidential; don't share them, including with classmates or colleagues
  • One account per person; don't register or attempt to register on someone else's behalf
  • Report a suspected compromised account to the School immediately

4. Acceptable Use

  • Don't attempt to access another user's scores, account, or personal data
  • Don't attempt to guess, brute-force, or otherwise bypass another user's PIN or password
  • Teachers and lecturers must only enter or edit scores for classes and sections they are actually assigned to
  • Don't use the discrepancy-flag feature in bad faith to harass or pressure a teacher

5. Accuracy of Academic Records

ResultTrack computes grades automatically from scores entered by teachers and lecturers. If a score appears incorrect, the discrepancy-flag feature is the official channel for requesting a review, not an informal request outside the platform. The School remains the final authority on official academic records.

6. Intellectual Property

The ResultTrack name, logo, and software are the property of the Fusion-circle team and are provided to the School for its use during the pilot. Nothing in these terms transfers ownership of the platform itself.

7. Disclaimers and Limitation of Liability

  • ResultTrack is provided as an MVP pilot. It is offered on an "as is" basis without guarantee of uninterrupted or error-free service
  • The School remains the authoritative source of record for all official academic purposes; ResultTrack is a tool for computing and displaying results, not a replacement for the School's own record-keeping obligations
  • To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Fusion-circle team is not liable for indirect or consequential loss arising from use of the platform during the pilot period

8. Termination

The School may discontinue use of ResultTrack at any time. An individual account may be suspended if these terms are violated, particularly the acceptable-use rules in Section 4.

9. Governing Law and Disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any dispute will first be addressed directly between the user and the School before any other action is taken.

10. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as ResultTrack moves from pilot to wider use. Users will be notified in-app at least 14 days before material changes take effect, giving time to raise concerns with the School before the changes apply.
