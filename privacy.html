Part 1 of the ResultTrack Privacy Policy & Terms and Conditions document, covering how ResultTrack and the pilot School handle personal data.

1. Who We Are

This policy covers ResultTrack as used by the pilot school ("the School"). The School is the data controller; ResultTrack is the platform the School uses to record and share academic results. Questions about this policy should go to the School's designated contact, named in Section 14.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Roster data: full name, year, department or class, and a verified contact (institutional or parent/guardian email or phone)
  • Registration data: activation channel used, timestamps of registration
  • Account data: registration or staff ID, hashed password, role
  • Academic data: CA scores, exam scores, computed final grades, the specific section and teacher tied to each score
  • Flag data: any discrepancy flag raised, its resolution, and any note attached
  • Audit data: a record of every change made to a score, who made it, and when

3. Why We Collect It

  • To provide the core service: computing and displaying accurate results (contractual necessity for staff and adult tertiary users; for minors, the basis is parental or guardian consent as described in Section 4, not contractual necessity, since a minor cannot independently enter into that contract)
  • To verify identity at registration and prevent one person accessing another's records (legitimate interest in account security)
  • To notify the right people when a score is finalized or a flag is raised (legitimate interest in service delivery)
  • To meet the School's own record-keeping obligations (legal obligation)

4. Children's Data

Many ResultTrack users are minors. The NDPA requires parental or guardian consent for processing the data of anyone under 18, treated with the same care as sensitive personal data, including reasonable measures to verify age. ResultTrack's registration design satisfies this directly rather than as an afterthought: secondary school registration is tied to a parent or guardian's verified contact, not the student's own, so a parent or guardian is the one confirming and consenting to account activation from the start. ResultTrack does not collect more data from a minor than necessary to provide the results service, and never uses minors' data for advertising or profiling.

5. Third Parties We Use

  • An analytics tool, used to measure whether ResultTrack is working as intended (for example, whether students see results promptly). This tool receives event data such as "a score was finalized" or "a flag was raised," not the underlying grade content itself where avoidable.
  • An email or SMS provider, used only to deliver activation PINs and password reset codes

6. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Nigerian law only permits transferring personal data outside Nigeria if the receiving country or organization provides a level of protection comparable to what the NDPA guarantees inside Nigeria. Once the School's analytics and messaging vendors are finalized, their hosting location will be confirmed and recorded here before launch. If a chosen vendor stores data outside Nigeria without adequate safeguards in place, an alternative vendor or a specific data protection agreement will be required first.

Vendor hosting locations are not yet finalized. This section will be updated with specifics before launch, per the text above.

7. How Long We Keep Data

Academic records are retained for as long as the School requires them for official purposes. Registration and account data is retained for the duration of a student's or staff member's association with the School. Audit logs are retained indefinitely, since their purpose is historical accuracy, not active use.

If the School stops using ResultTrack entirely, the School's data will be exported and returned to the School in a usable format within 30 days of that decision, then deleted from ResultTrack's systems within a further 30 days, except where a copy must be retained to comply with a legal obligation.

8. Your Rights

  • Right to see your own data: students and staff can view their own academic records directly in the product at any time
  • Right to correct inaccurate data: the in-product discrepancy-flag feature is the primary mechanism for this, routing directly to the teacher who entered it
  • Right to withdraw consent, where consent is the basis for processing your data, without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out
  • Right to lodge a complaint directly with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled and it has not been resolved through the School
  • Any other rights recognized for data subjects under the NDPA; contact the School's designated contact in Section 14 to exercise any of these

9. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text
  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • Activation PINs and password reset codes expire after 15 minutes if unused
  • Repeated failed login attempts trigger a delay before another attempt is allowed
  • Every change to a score produces an audit log entry, without exception

10. Data Breach Notification

If a breach occurs that is likely to pose a high risk to any user's rights, the School will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and will notify affected users directly with clear instructions on what to do. A breach register recording the facts, consequences, and corrective action taken for every breach is maintained internally, in line with NDPA requirements.

11. Data Protection Officer

Organizations processing personal data for more than 200 people within any 6-month period are required under the NDPA to appoint a Data Protection Officer. The School commits to assessing its enrollment against this threshold within the first month of the pilot. If the threshold is met, or expected to be met, a Data Protection Officer will be appointed within 30 days of that determination and named in Section 14.

12. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), which together set out the current data protection framework in Nigeria. Provisions relating to minors are read alongside the Child Rights Act 2003.

13. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data is collected or how it's used, users will be notified in-app before the change takes effect.

14. Contact

School's designated data contact name, role, and email to be added before pilot launch. If a Data Protection Officer is appointed per Section 11, their contact replaces or supplements this entry.
